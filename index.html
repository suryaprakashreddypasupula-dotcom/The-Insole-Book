Welcome to Hike Medical — Custom Orthotic Training

Welcome to one of the most exciting places in modern healthcare manufacturing! Hike Medical is on a bold mission — making custom foot orthotics faster, smarter, and more accessible than ever before. With over 600 printers running 24/7 at the largest orthotic 3D print farm in the United States, we're turning a process that once took weeks into something that ships out of our facility in just five business days. That's not just innovation — that's real impact on real lives.

The platform is built so a clinic can evaluate, scan, and order in 15 minutes or less. No extra hardware — any phone with a camera is enough. Evaluation, scanning, documentation, and ordering live in one workflow. And 3D-printed production holds geometry tight enough that first-time fit sits at 99%.

This guide exists because at Hike, product knowledge isn't just for one team — it belongs to everyone. Understanding what we make, how we make it, and why it matters is what connects every person here to the bigger mission. From insole types and materials, to accommodations, modifications, production flow, and quality standards — this is the foundation we all share. Because when everyone knows the product inside and out, the whole company moves better. One step at a time. 🦶
